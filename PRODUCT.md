# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A teacher (French-speaking school) working mostly on a laptop, in class and for planning afterwards. During a lesson they need quick lookups (who is this student, where do they sit, what are the difficulties); outside lessons they do admin: class lists, evaluations, timetables, reports. One teacher today, possibly more staff later. Not technical.

## Product Purpose

Student management tool: classes and students (with photos), evaluations, seating plans, timetables, school calendar, analytics and exportable reports. Success is a teacher who finds any student or class in two clicks, enters evaluations fast, and never has to guess what a screen or button does.

## Brand Personality

Clean, efficient, trustworthy. A crisp workspace that favours fast scanning and low effort over decoration. Calm confidence, plain French labels, no jargon.

## Anti-references

- The generic blue SaaS dashboard: blue accent, white cards, identical stat tiles.
- Dark and techy terminal looks.
- Cluttered admin panels with dense tables, tiny text, and too many buttons.
- Childish or cartoon styling; this is a professional tool.

## Design Principles

1. Scan first: the most-needed information on a screen is visible without clicking or scrolling.
2. One obvious next action per screen; secondary actions step back.
3. Plain French, verb-led labels that say exactly what will happen.
4. Every empty, loading and error state tells the teacher what to do next.
5. Quiet by default, colour only where it carries meaning (status, difficulty, selection).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Assumed baseline (not yet confirmed by the user): WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, full keyboard operation, visible focus, respect for reduced motion, status never conveyed by colour alone.
